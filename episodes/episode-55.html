Is the Pointing Lab the Most Versatile Dog on the Planet? — Troy Benson & the American Pointing Lab Association

Hosted by Tyce Erickson — professional bird dog trainer and owner of Utah Bird Dog Training.

Apple Podcasts Spotify

About This Episode

Tyce sits down with Troy Benson, President of the American Pointing Labrador Association (APLA), for a deep dive into pointing labs and everything the APLA has built around them.

Troy is a professional trainer out of Minnesota (North Star Pointing Labs), a pheasant hunting guide in South Dakota, and one of the architects behind the APLA's national event — the Triple Crown. He's been involved in the pointing lab world for decades, from running his first certified dog to judging the Triple Crown three years in a row before finally competing himself last year.

In this episode they cover:

  • What a pointing lab is — and where the instinct likely comes from
  • The APLA title levels: Certified, Advanced (APR), Master, and Grand Master
  • The Triple Crown national event and how to qualify
  • Why the biggest training jump is Certified to Advanced, not Master to Grand Master
  • How pointing labs are retrievers first — and why that shapes how you develop the point
  • The APLA Hall of Fame and what it takes to get a dog in

Whether you already own a pointing lab, are considering one, or just want to understand what makes these dogs so versatile in the field, this one's worth a listen.

Tyce breeds pointing labs at Utah Pointing Labs and works with bird dog owners through Utah Bird Dog Training.

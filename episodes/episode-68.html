Pheasants Forever with Jared Wiklund: Pheasant Hatch, Drought, Winter Cover & Public Access (PATH)

Hosted by Tyce Erickson — professional bird dog trainer and owner of Utah Bird Dog Training.

Apple Podcasts Spotify

About This Episode

Jared Wiklund, Director of Communications for Pheasants Forever and Quail Forever, joins Tyce to talk about why pheasant numbers rise and fall and what it takes to keep upland habitat on the landscape. Jared shares how Pheasants Forever started with a newspaper column in St. Paul, Minnesota, and how a year-long unpaid PR internship turned into 16 years with the organization. Then they get into the biology: why drought hits pheasant chicks through the loss of soft-bodied insects, why a cold, wet hatch can wipe out broods, why pheasants only nest once a year, and why predation is the number one killer with weather a close second. They cover winter cover and why "pheasants don't grow in trees," the South Dakota CREP, the new Public Access to Habitat (PATH) program and its onX layer, strategic land acquisition, why Utah has fewer pheasants than the Dakotas, the huge sagebrush restoration work in Idaho, and how listeners can find a chapter or start one.

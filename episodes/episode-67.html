About This Episode

Tyce Erickson sits down with his little brother Dane Erickson and his friend Matt Bingham, owner of Pine Pest Control in Mona, Utah, to tell the story of their recent mule deer hunt. Matt tells how a preschool-age fascination with mule deer turned into a lifelong hunt for big bucks, and Tyce and Dane share how they grew up hunting with their dad. Then they walk through the trip: how the tags and the llamas came together, a six-mile pack-in with eight llamas (and some "llama drama" with Lewis), a midnight camp, and a morning of glassing big basins. They talk through Tyce's son Crew's dream about a buck and the shot that followed, the "no ground shrinkage" walk-up, a pair of sparring bucks that got away, a missed shot from an over-dialed scope, and Matt's five misses with a turret that was not zeroed. The night a 50 to 60 mph storm blew Matt's teepee away, Matt's last-day buck with his new 7 PRC, the missing llama on the hike out and Tyce's big buck wrap it up, along with the mindset lessons behind the hunt.

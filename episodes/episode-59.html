About This Episode

In this episode, I sit down with Scooter Cooper and Dylan Stamper from NOK — “Never Out Killed.”

We talk about their dogs, how NOK got started, the business behind the brand, and some of the awesome hunts they’ve been fortunate enough to experience along the way.

Scooter and Dylan are good dudes who love hunting, dogs, and the outdoors, and I think you’re going to enjoy this conversation.
